Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This notice explains the website's current data practices. It does not mean that all applicable regulatory registrations or permits have been completed.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

Amira Abdel-Fadil, an independent filmmaker and visual artist based in Cairo, Egypt, is responsible for personal information processed through this website. For privacy enquiries or rights requests, email byamira01@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Newsletter: your email address, selected language, subscription status, date and version of consent, and technical information used to limit abusive signups.
  • Contact and pitches: information you choose to send by email or through an available contact or submission channel.
  • Accounts: identity and authentication data needed by authorized editors and, if activated, educational members. Student activity may include enrollments, assignments, submissions and feedback.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and access logs held by hosting and security services to deliver and protect the site.
  • Preferences: your light/dark theme and privacy consent choices saved in your browser.
  • Optional analytics: if you agree, Google Analytics may process page visits, event information and browser or device data under its terms and settings.

3. Why we use this information

We use information to operate the portfolio, respond to enquiries, deliver requested newsletters, secure accounts, prevent misuse, administer approved education access and, with permission, understand website usage. Where the law requires a legal basis, this may be consent (newsletters and optional analytics), provision of a requested service (accounts and communications), legitimate interests or an applicable equivalent for security, and legal obligations.

4. Newsletter and marketing choices

Signing up for Field Notes is voluntary and requires a separate consent action. You can withdraw at any time through an unsubscribe link where provided or by contacting the email address above. Unsubscribe records may be retained as necessary to honor your choice and prevent unwanted future email.

5. Cookies, local storage and analytics

Essential browser storage remembers preferences and supports login where available. Optional Google Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics only start after you consent through our privacy controls. Account, recovery and unsubscribe pages do not load optional analytics. You may decline or change your choice via the Cookie Policy and the Privacy settings control in the footer. This does not disable strictly necessary storage. Independently embedded external videos or links may have their own privacy terms.

6. Service providers and international processing

Site infrastructure includes hosting and security services such as Cloudflare, a content repository and deployment workflow on GitHub, and Supabase for authentication, database and storage. Collaboration and story-pitch forms use Tally only when you choose to open them; Tally receives form data you submit and may receive technical connection data. Google receives analytics data only if you opt in. Service providers may process data outside Egypt, subject to applicable contractual, technical and legal safeguards. Email delivery and media providers may process the information required to provide their services. Third-party privacy policies also apply when you follow their links.

7. Sharing and publication

We do not sell your personal information. We share data with necessary service providers and, when legally required, competent authorities. Materials that you submit for possible publication are not automatically authorized for publication; usage and credits should be agreed separately. Please avoid sending sensitive information unless necessary and agreed.

8. How long we keep data

Newsletter details are kept while the subscription remains active and, if you unsubscribe, the minimum suppression information reasonably necessary to respect that request. Correspondence and submissions are kept for the period needed to respond, manage the creative relationship or meet legal duties. Accounts and learning records are kept while needed for the authorized service and applicable obligations. Hosting and security logs follow provider and operational retention schedules. Contact us to ask about the period for a particular record.

9. Security

Access controls, restricted databases, encryption in transit and operational safeguards are used to protect data. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Please do not email passwords or confidential access tokens.

10. Your rights

Depending on the law applicable to you, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or an objection to processing, and you may withdraw consent. To exercise a right, email byamira01@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information when lawfully required. You may also contact the competent data-protection authority where applicable.

11. Children

The public portfolio is not designed to collect personal data directly from children. Any future educational enrollment involving minors requires additional age-appropriate and parental or guardian safeguards where the law requires them.

12. Updates and contact

This notice may change as services and legal obligations develop. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised date. Privacy enquiries: byamira01@gmail.com.
