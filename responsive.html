About Amira.

Amira Abdel-Fadil has always been drawn to the things people carry quietly: a grandmother’s recipes, the green that grows between buildings, the small moments that hold a whole life.

A filmmaker, writer and photographer from Cairo, she lets each story decide its own pace, and she is still learning to listen well enough to follow it.
