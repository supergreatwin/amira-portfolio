About Amira.

Amira Abdel-Fadil has always been drawn to the things people carry quietly: a grandmother’s recipes, the green that grows between buildings, the small moments that hold a whole life. A filmmaker, writer and photographer from Cairo, she lets each story decide its own pace, and she is still learning to listen well enough to follow it.

Her path began in digital media, where her articles and edited videos reached audiences of more than a million. Event coordination, teaching and volunteering shaped her too, and she credits them with teaching her patience and care for the people on the other side of the lens.

Her documentaries have been recognized and screened at international festivals, and she is grateful for every audience that has stayed to watch. What she hopes to make, in the end, is not a film but a feeling: an invitation to look inward, and to step back into a world that feels a little closer than it seemed, a little more tender, and a little more open to everyone in it.

Letter of recommendation · 22 November 2026

To Whom It May Concern,

I am pleased to recommend Amira Abdel-Fadil, with whom I worked during the organisation and delivery of the Global Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) Summit, held in Cairo, Egypt, from 1 to 3 October 2024.

The Summit brought together approximately 700 delegates from 101 countries, including senior government officials, representatives of international organisations, development partners and technology leaders. Delivering an event of this scale required considerable coordination, attention to detail and the ability to respond effectively to changing requirements.

Amira was involved in delegate relations and logistics, two areas that were particularly important to the successful delivery of the Summit. Her responsibilities involved supporting delegate coordination, attending to logistical requirements and helping ensure participants had a positive experience throughout the event.

Throughout our engagement, I found Amira to be professional, dependable and attentive to her responsibilities. She approached her work with a constructive attitude, communicated well with colleagues and delegates, and demonstrated the composure needed when working under the pressures of a major international event. She was also willing to take initiative and contribute wherever additional support was required.

What I particularly appreciated was her ability to remain focused on the practical details while maintaining a courteous and helpful approach to the people she was supporting. These qualities are important in international event management, where the quality of delegate engagement can have a significant bearing on the overall experience.

Amira made a valuable contribution to the successful delivery of the Global DPI Summit. Based on my experience working with her, I would be happy to recommend her for roles involving event coordination, delegate management, logistics and stakeholder relations.

I wish her continued success in her professional career.

Yours faithfully,

Garikai Nhongo
Summit Curator and Coordinator — Global DPI Summit 2024
