Cookie & Storage Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. What this policy covers

Cookies are small pieces of data stored by a browser. Similar technologies, including local storage, can remember choices and account sessions. This policy describes the technologies used by Amira's portfolio, including optional analytics. Some third-party media services may also operate their own technologies if you interact with their content.

2. Essential storage

The website stores necessary settings so core functions work reliably. These are not used for optional advertising or analytics. You can clear them through browser settings, but doing so may sign you out or reset choices.

3. Optional Google Analytics

On the official domain, the website can load Google Analytics 4 after you choose Accept analytics. It may then set cookies such as _ga and measurement-specific _ga_*, subject to Google's implementation. These help assess page visits and site usage. Analytics is off by default and is not required to use the portfolio. The website does not intentionally request advertising personalization consent.

4. How to manage or withdraw consent

Choose Reject optional, Accept analytics, or Customize in the initial banner. After making a choice, use the Privacy settings control in the footer or below to review and change it. Withdrawing optional analytics permission stops future loading and reloads the page if necessary to stop a previously loaded analytics script. We also attempt to clear accessible Google Analytics cookies; you may clear browser storage for further control.

5. Embedded content and external links

Embedded video players and externally linked services may set their own cookies when you load or interact with them. Review their policies before use. This website's analytics preference does not control cookies created by independent external websites.

6. Questions and updates

For privacy questions, contact byamira01@gmail.com. Related details are in the Privacy Policy. This policy may be updated if the technology or applicable law changes.
